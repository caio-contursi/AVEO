AVEO PITCH v0.4 — 13 OCT 2026
Speaker: Bruno. The closing slide presents the team's collective contribution.

SLIDE 1

Hi, I'm Bruno. I joined my son and three other students to build Aveo around a simple question: when a transfer is blocked, can the operator explain why? My compliance experience shaped that question. We haven't yet documented a real operator case, so customer discovery is a gate, not a claim.

SLIDE 2

A script and an explorer can expose the same underlying data. Our bet is a repeatable workflow: explain the cause, group assets sharing a proof, and verify recovery from fresh state. We're not replacing Tokeny's broader tooling. We need to measure whether this Solana-focused workflow saves operators time and errors.

SLIDE 3

Why Solana? SAS provides reusable attestations, and Token-2022 lets the hook enforce rules during transfers, beyond the Desk. The idea could also work with ERC-3643. But this implementation would need new proof readers, transaction builders and verification adapters. We're not claiming effortless portability or an existing EVM integration.

SLIDE 4

Our initial customer hypothesis is a platform managing several permissioned assets on Solana. Operations and compliance teams would use it; a head of operations is one budget-owner hypothesis to test. We plan subscriptions and integration services. Price comes after workflow and budget validation, starting with a paid pilot proposal.

SLIDE 5

The program, test accounts and public Desk are deployed on devnet. You can inspect live eligibility without signing. But our full wallet and six-scene acceptance are not complete, and a visitor's wallet needs provisioning. We will not claim an unassisted two-minute recovery before a newcomer actually completes it.

SLIDE 6

We're a team combining twenty years of audit and compliance experience, a Computer Science background, and four Information Systems students. Together, we've built the program, integrations and Desk, using AI tools along the way. Building across generations gives this a personal purpose, too. Our ambition is simple: help teams understand restrictions, not just enforce them. Explore Aveo, inspect the evidence, and help us shape what comes next.
